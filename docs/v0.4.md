# EchoGuide 0.4 spatial scan lab
Fixed 1–16 rows/columns, defaults 6×8, 60°×45°, 20 ms slots and 15 ms pulse. Column-major top to bottom, optional row/snake. Manual PING one frame; auto repeats; Stop flushes AudioTrack. No delay, reference sound or synchronization sound. Native copies a complete packet once per frame; silent cells occupy exactly their time slots. Binaural effects reset per slot and tail is faded/truncated at boundary (may reduce very short slot localization). 48 kHz 512-frame native blocks; variable slots use a prepared stereo slot buffer. 
Kotlin triangle meshes/ray intersection, 12 scene presets and empty; manual single/multi/all cell editing including independent normal angles relative to facing listener, distance, roughness, radial velocity, enabled and validity. Hard cutoff or fade, gain applied once. Optional HRTF, distance, brightness, roughness, Doppler; noise/click textures do not provide stable pitch and thus Doppler is best tested with harmonics/chirp. JSON save/load including grid and DSP (SOFA path not portable). Shape blind practice and horizontal direction, distance, brightness, roughness, Doppler A/B; CSV results, adjustable differences, trial times include playback/replay. Not clinical threshold estimation. No camera or real navigation.
